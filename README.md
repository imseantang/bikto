# Bikto

**Bikto** is a URL shortener, QR code generator and link in bio tool with click analytics. It's built for indie makers, content creators, small businesses and marketing teams.

Create short links on `bik.to`, make QR codes that you can still edit after printing, publish a link in bio page and see where your clicks come from, all in one workspace.

Website: [https://bikto.app](https://bikto.app)

---

## What Is Bikto?

Bikto is a link management service for people who share links in public: on social profiles, printed flyers, packaging, business cards, emails and ads.

Instead of spreading this work across several single-purpose tools, Bikto puts it in one place:

- Shorten URLs with custom short codes
- Change a link's destination after you've shared it
- Generate static QR codes for free, without an account
- Create trackable QR codes that can be edited after printing
- Protect any short link with a password
- Publish a link in bio page at `bik.to/@name`
- Route visitors by device or country
- Track clicks and scans by country, city, device, browser and referrer

Links you share keep working on every plan, including Free. Short codes are never released or reassigned to anyone else.

## Key Features

### URL Shortener

Create short links on `bik.to` with custom codes. You can change where a link points at any time: the destination updates and the short link stays the same, so links you've already shared don't need to be replaced.

[https://bikto.app/url-shortener](https://bikto.app/url-shortener)

### QR Code Generator

Generate static QR codes for free, with no account needed. Supported types:

- [Website / URL](https://bikto.app/qr-code-generator)
- [Wi-Fi](https://bikto.app/qr-code-generator/wifi): guests scan to join your network instead of typing the password
- [Contact (vCard)](https://bikto.app/qr-code-generator/vcard): name, phone, email, company and website saved to contacts in one scan
- [Email](https://bikto.app/qr-code-generator/email): opens a new email with the address, subject and message filled in
- [Phone](https://bikto.app/qr-code-generator/phone): offers to call your number when scanned
- [SMS](https://bikto.app/qr-code-generator/sms): opens a text message with the message already written
- [Text](https://bikto.app/qr-code-generator/text): shows plain text when scanned

### Trackable (Dynamic) QR Codes

A trackable QR code points to a `bik.to` address, so you can change its destination after printing and every scan is counted. Paid plans can add a logo to trackable QR codes.

### Password-Protected Links

You can password-protect any short link on every plan, including Free. Visitors, including people who scan a trackable QR code, have to enter the password before they're redirected. The destination stays hidden until then.

### Link in Bio

Each account can publish a link in bio page at `bik.to/@name`, with templates, social icons, a QR code and click statistics.

[https://bikto.app/link-in-bio](https://bikto.app/link-in-bio)

### Link Analytics

See where clicks and scans come from and how traffic changes over time, broken down by country, city, device, browser and referrer.

[https://bikto.app/link-analytics](https://bikto.app/link-analytics)

### Device and Country Routing

Send visitors to different destinations depending on their device or country, all from the same short link.

### Custom Domains, Teams and API

Paid plans add custom domains, multiple workspaces, team members, link import, daily backups, a REST API and MCP access.

## Why Bikto?

A printed QR code or a link in a published post is hard to take back. If the destination changes, or the service behind it shuts the link off, everything already out in the world breaks.

Bikto is built so that the links you share keep working:

- Destinations can be edited without changing the link
- Links keep redirecting on every plan, including Free
- Short codes are never recycled
- Clicks, scans and edits aren't capped
